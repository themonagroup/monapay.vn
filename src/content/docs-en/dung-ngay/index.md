MONA Pay was originally built for developers wiring webhooks and an API into a website. Most restaurants, salons, Facebook or Zalo shops, and small classes have nobody who codes and no website either. This page is for them: no website, no code, just three steps below.

## Who this is for

- **Restaurants and drink shops:** the cashier needs to know a transfer landed, without asking the owner "did the money come in yet".
- **Salons and spas:** a customer books and pays a deposit by transfer, and the receptionist needs to confirm the slot right away.
- **Facebook and Zalo shops:** a customer says "I sent it" but nobody has actually confirmed the money arrived, and there is no website to attach a payment tool to.
- **Small classes and training centres:** parents or students pay tuition by transfer, and staff need to know who has paid without combing through a bank statement.

## What you need first

1. **A bank account in your own name** (personal or business), where customers will send money. MONA Pay currently connects ACB, with other banks in the process of connecting.
2. **The phone with the number registered to that bank account**, since connecting the bank requires confirming with an OTP code.

With both of those, the three steps below take about 10 minutes.

## 3 core steps

### 1. Connect your bank account

Create an account at [my.monapay.vn](https://my.monapay.vn), it activates immediately. Under Bank, enter the account number and the phone number registered with the bank. The bank sends two OTP codes in turn: one to create the connection, one to turn on transaction alerts.

### 2. Pick an alert channel

- **A Zalo or Telegram group:** add the MONA Pay bot to a group of 5 to 7 staff, so everyone sees the same alert the moment money arrives.
- **A read-aloud screen at the counter:** open a link on a phone or tablet at the counter, tap once to enable audio, and every new payment is read out loud with the amount and note.
- **A verified email address:** for an owner who wants to track payments alone.

### 3. Print a QR code or grab a payment link

Print a bank QR code for the counter so customers scan and pay the right account without copying a number by hand, or grab a payment link to send over chat when customers are not on site.
